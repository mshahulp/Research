Dear Editors,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript, "A Rate–Distortion Framework for
Language-Model Scaling Laws: Entropy Floor, Two-Bottleneck Exponents, and the
Joint Law," for consideration for publication in Entropy (Special Issue on the
Information-Theoretic Foundations and Applications of Learning).

The empirical scaling law L(N,D) = E + A N^(-alpha_N) + B D^(-alpha_D) is the
central quantitative regularity of modern language models, but existing theory
either treats the floor E as a free parameter or derives only upper bounds
without identifying the irreducible term. Our contribution is to close this gap
using the exact rate-distortion identity R(D) = H(Y|X) - D for log-loss.

Specifically, we:
  (i)   identify the entropy floor E as the conditional entropy H(Y|X), a
        computable quantity;
  (ii)  derive a capacity-limited model-size exponent
        alpha_N = gamma(2s/beta_reg - 1) with a boundary-degeneracy correction;
  (iii) derive a data-limited exponent alpha_D = gamma_ent/(2 beta_corr) from
        temporal corpus statistics, subsuming the recent result of Cagnetta et
        al.; and
  (iv)  obtain a joint law with a vanishing cross term and self-truncating
        variance, yielding two compute-optimality predictions.

A distinctive feature of this work is its scientific candor. Rather than
present a purely confirmatory account, we report an explicit empirical
identifiability audit of the spectral quantities that enter the formulas. We
include a pre-registered negative result (the code/prose boundary-degeneracy
ordering was contradicted by our ladder test), and we use a synthetic-control
experiment to show that part of the observed spectral failure is
measurement-confounded. We believe this combination of an exact theory with a
transparent, non-circular empirical assessment is of high relevance to Entropy's
readership.

All data, code, and pre-registration materials are publicly available in the
companion repository, and the Supporting Information contains full details.

We confirm that this manuscript is original, has not been published elsewhere,
and is not under consideration by another journal. All authors have approved
the manuscript and agree with its submission to Entropy.
