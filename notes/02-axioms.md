## Standing requirements for every axiom set

- Independence: no axiom derivable from the others.
- Necessity: removing any axiom breaks some essential property.
- Minimality: no redundant axiom.
- Meaningfulness: each axiom has an operational interpretation.
- Forbidden: axioms that silently encode arbitrary weighting decisions
  (e.g., a fixed exchange rate between quality and energy) without defense.
